About Rheumatology

Rheumatology focuses on diagnosing and treating autoimmune and inflammatory disorders affecting joints, muscles, and connective tissues. We manage rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, lupus, gout, spondyloarthropathies, and vasculitis.

Our team provides early diagnosis, advanced medical therapies including biologics, and coordinated rehabilitation to improve mobility and quality of life.
