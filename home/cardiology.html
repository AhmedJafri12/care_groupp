About Cardiology

Our Cardiology Department manages coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, valvular disorders, and hypertension. We offer non-invasive imaging (ECHO, TMT, Holter), cardiac catheterization, angioplasty, and pacemaker/ICD implantation.

We emphasize prevention through risk-factor control, lifestyle counselling, and cardiac rehab, working closely with endocrinology and nutrition teams for comprehensive care.
